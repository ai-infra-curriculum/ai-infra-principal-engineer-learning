# Role Requirements: Principal AI Infrastructure Engineer

**Level:** 5
**Experience Level:** 10-15+ years in software engineering, infrastructure, or related fields, with demonstrated technical leadership and innovation
**Market Demand:** Critical Shortage - Extremely limited pool of principal-level IC talent. High competition among FAANG, major tech companies, and well-funded startups. Essential for companies with complex AI infrastructure challenges. Often recruited internally from senior engineering ranks or from other top tech companies.
**Salary Range (US, 2025):** $240,000 - $600,000 (median $330,000)

- Provide technical leadership for critical AI infrastructure initiatives
- Design and drive implementation of innovative infrastructure solutions
- Lead technical strategy for AI infrastructure across multiple teams
- Conduct research and develop proofs-of-concept for emerging technologies
- Make critical architectural and technical decisions for the organization
- Mentor principal and senior engineers across the company
- Drive technical excellence and engineering best practices
- Lead cross-team technical integrations and platform initiatives
- Solve the most complex and ambiguous technical challenges
- Represent company in technical communities and open source
- Define and evolve engineering standards and guidelines
- Drive innovation through R&D and experimentation
- Review and influence major technical decisions across organization
- Build and lead technical working groups and communities of practice
- Establish technical vision aligned with business objectives
