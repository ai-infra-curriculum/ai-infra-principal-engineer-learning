# Interview Readiness Guide – AI Infrastructure Principal Engineer

## Purpose
Turn principal-level modules into interview narratives that demonstrate technical leadership, multi-team influence, and strategic decision-making.

## Core Competencies to Highlight
- Portfolio-level technical direction and decision logs  
- Balancing innovation, risk, and cost across squads  
- Mentoring staff engineers and standardising best practices  
- Translating architectural vision into measurable outcomes

## Preparation Checklist
1. Select three decision logs or major initiatives that show cross-team impact.  
2. Prepare metrics illustrating success (cost savings, reliability gains, delivery speed).  
3. Document how you resolved conflicts or negotiated trade-offs with leadership.  
4. Practice articulating future vision and succession planning.

## Mock Interview Structure (60 minutes)
| Segment | Objective | Repo Materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | Principal journey overview | Root README |
| 15 min | Technical strategy deep dive | `projects/` decision frameworks |
| 15 min | Org-level influence story | Coaching/mentorship sections |
| 10 min | Scenario: competing initiatives or budget constraints | Decision logs |
| 10 min | Candidate questions | List below |

## Sample Technical Prompts
- “Describe a time you unified multiple infrastructure teams under a single roadmap.”  
- “How do you evaluate and communicate the technical debt you’re willing to carry?”  
- “Walk through a major infrastructure investment decision and its outcomes.”

## Behavioral Prompts
- Handling misalignment between exec priorities and engineering reality  
- Coaching engineers through ambiguity or failure  
- Scaling your influence without micromanaging

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer
- “How do principal engineers influence product strategy here?”  
- “What metrics or narratives resonate with your executive team?”  
- “How are technical standards established and enforced across squads?”

## Next Steps
